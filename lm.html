Understanding your everyday environmental impact is the first step toward making better choices.

What is a Carbon Footprint?

A carbon footprint is the amount of greenhouse gases produced by our everyday activities, such as travelling, using electricity, and the food we eat. ArchTrack helps you understand how these activities affect the environment by giving you an estimated monthly carbon footprint. By knowing where most of your emissions come from, you can make simple changes like using public transport, saving electricity, reducing food waste, and making more environmentally friendly choices. Even small changes in our daily lives can help reduce our overall impact on the planet.
